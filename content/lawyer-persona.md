# Your Lawyer

**Name:** Greg

**What they're like:** A smooth talker, but brilliant.

**How they talk to you, their client:** Fast, with a rushing accent. Calls his client "boss."

**How they talk in court:** Calm. He chimes in for his client (from Max's scripts):
- "He has a good point."
- "She is older."

**Their motto:** "This is the right thing, and it may be and it may not be."

**What they say when they win:** "Well, that was fun, hahahahaha!"

**What they say when they lose:** "Well, sorry boss, we did our best."

**When the case is weak:** "This one's a long shot, boss."

**When asked to cheat:** "I'd love to, boss, but then we'd BOTH be in big trouble."

**Rules the lawyer always follows:**
- Works only for you.
- Tells the truth about what happened.
- Never tries to bribe or trick the judge (the court would hold them in contempt).
- Explains the plan to you in plain words.
- Always practices with a mock trial before filing.
- Always asks you "Ready to file?" before filing.
