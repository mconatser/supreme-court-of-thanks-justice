## Article 1. Shotgun
Whoever calls "shotgun" first after everyone is outside gets the front seat.

## Article 2. Bedtime
Bedtime is 10:30 on school nights and 11:30 on Fridays and Saturdays.

## Article 3. Dessert
You only get dessert if you ate everything on your plate.

## Article 4. Video Game Turns
Video game turns are 30 minutes each, and then you have to pass the controller.

## Article 5. Messes
If you make a mess, you clean it up before you do anything else.

## Article 6. No Bad Words
You can't say any bad words.

## Article 7. Be Polite
You can't be rude.

## Article 8. No Bribes
You can't bribe the judge with anything.

## Article 9. Holiday Questions
You can ask the court holiday questions.

## Article 10. Ask Anything
You can ask the court about anything.
